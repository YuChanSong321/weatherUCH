Values for the current eccentricity. Insolation from the inverse-square law; temperature from a zero-dimensional energy balance with an ice–albedo feedback — illustrative, not a GCM.
Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · Space pause · 1/2 view · R reset

Distances and body sizes are compressed for legibility; the orbital mechanics (Kepler's 1st and 2nd laws) and the axial tilt are solved properly. All three dials drift on their own clocks: eccentricity between about 0.005 and 0.058 over ~100,000 years, tilt between 22.1° and 24.5° over ~41,000, and the perihelion works its way around the calendar in ~26,000. That is the Milankovitch cycle.
